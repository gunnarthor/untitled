Freelance Work Opportunity

ReinforceLabs is looking for a freelance Rails developer to join the team

About us

At ReinforceLabs we work with clients from around the world, across different industries. Our specialty is the insurance sector.

We're a small, experienced team that values craftsmanship and professionalism. We care about building software that is maintainable, readable and well-tested — and we look for people who share that mindset.

The first project

A large, up-to-date application used by insurance brokers in several European countries. Built on Rails 8 with a modern stack.

Ruby on Rails 8 PostgreSQL Sidekiq Hotwire (Turbo + Stimulus) ViewComponent Tailwind CSS

What we're looking for

  • Solid experience with Ruby on Rails (5+ years ideally)
  • Previous experience working as a freelancer/contractor
  • Ability to work autonomously writing high-quality, well-tested code
  • Comfort with mostly asynchronous work
  • Good level of English (our language of communication)
  • Comfortable using AI tools to accelerate development while taking full ownership of the code and its quality

Terms

Starting part-time, approximately 20 hours per week, with the possibility of expanding hours and long-term continuity.

If you're interested or know someone who'd be a good fit, reach out at contact@reinforcelabs.com.
