Senior Software Engineer

Full-time · Remote · Ruby on Rails

About us

At ReinforceLabs, we work exclusively in insurance, partnering with brokers, MGAs, and insurers to solve the complex operational problems.

We're a tight-knit, experienced team that values craftsmanship and professionalism. We care about building software that is maintainable, readable and well-tested, and we look for people who share that mindset.

The role

You'll work across our insurance platforms: broker systems, rating engines, claims, and more. Our stack is modern and evolves as needed:

Ruby on Rails 8 PostgreSQL Sidekiq Hotwire (Turbo + Stimulus) ViewComponent Tailwind CSS

How we work

  • We support each other and ask for help early instead of getting stuck
  • Proactive communication, get ahead of problems before they grow
  • Default to async, but jump on a call when it's faster
  • Organized to meet deadlines without the stress
  • Continuous learning and improvement are encouraged

What we're looking for

  • 7+ years of professional software engineering experience
  • Solid experience with Ruby on Rails
  • Ability to work autonomously, owning projects end to end
  • Strong communication skills
  • Experienced working remotely
  • Strong English, our default language of communication
  • Comfortable using AI tools to accelerate development while taking full ownership of the code and its quality

Terms

Full-time, remote-first position.

We start with an initial 2-week trial period at 20 hours per week.

ReinforceLabs is registered in the UK. You'll invoice us for your work each month, rather than being on payroll.

Ready to apply, or have questions first? Either way, just email us.
